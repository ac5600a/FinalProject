From: Daniel Mercer, VP of Sales, Apex Technologies To: Laura Bennett, Chief Commercial Officer, Apex Technologies CC: Regional Sales Directors Date: February 12, 2024 Subject: Q2 Enterprise Pricing Strategy
Laura,
Ahead of our Q2 planning meeting, I wanted to flag some concerns about where we are positioning our enterprise pricing. Our sales teams are continuing to offer substantial discounts to win large accounts, and I am concerned that we are sacrificing too much margin.
I've noticed that Meridian and Northstar have also been emphasizing value-based pricing in recent industry presentations. It may be worth considering whether we are undervaluing our product relative to the market.
I recommend that we establish clearer internal pricing guidelines and require additional approval for discounts exceeding 15%. We should also review our pricing strategy for the largest enterprise accounts before the next quarter.
Let me know your thoughts.
Daniel
