From: Michael Reeves, VP of Sales, Meridian SystemsTo: Jennifer Walsh, Chief Financial Officer, Meridian SystemsCC: Meridian Regional Sales DirectorsDate: November 3, 2025Subject: Q4 Sales Performance and Pricing
Jennifer,
I reviewed the Q4 pipeline and wanted to provide some context for our recent pricing decisions.
We have continued to lose opportunities to Northstar and Apex in several key accounts, particularly where customers prioritize implementation speed and integration capabilities over price.
Our recent decision to reduce discounting was driven primarily by margin concerns and the costs associated with supporting customized implementations. We have also seen several customers push back against our pricing, so I would not characterize the market as moving uniformly in one direction.
For next quarter, I recommend that we continue evaluating pricing on an account-by-account basis and avoid applying a blanket discounting policy across the sales organization.
Michael
