From: Robert Chen, Chief Revenue Officer, Northstar Digital Solutions To: Amanda Foster, Chief Executive Officer, Northstar Digital Solutions Date: December 8, 2025 Subject: Enterprise Pricing Review
Amanda,
I wanted to summarize the results of our recent enterprise pricing review.
Our decision to increase certain implementation and support fees was based on rising labor costs and the additional resources required for large-scale deployments. We have also identified several accounts where our previous pricing did not adequately reflect the complexity of the services provided.
We should expect some customers to push back, particularly those who have received more competitive proposals from Apex and Meridian.
I recommend that we continue using our own cost and profitability analyses when setting prices. We should not assume that our competitors will make similar decisions, especially given the differences in their product offerings and customer bases.
Robert
