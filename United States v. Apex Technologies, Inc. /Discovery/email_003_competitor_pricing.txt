From: Laura Bennett, Chief Commercial Officer, Apex Technologies To: Daniel Mercer, VP of Sales, Apex Technologies Date: October 3, 2024 Subject: Competitor Pricing Trends
Daniel,
Following the industry conference last week, I wanted to discuss the pricing trends we have been seeing across the enterprise software market.
Several competitors appear to be moving away from aggressive discounting, particularly for larger corporate customers. Our sales team has also reported that customers are increasingly expecting premium implementation and support services.
I think we should evaluate whether our current discounting practices remain sustainable. We need to be careful not to make assumptions about competitors' strategies, but the broader market conditions may justify a change in our approach.
Please ask the finance team to prepare an analysis of our current margins and discounting practices before we make any changes.
Laura
